# Certify step: receipt packaging fix (2026-10-29)

This fixes the defect recorded in `CERTIFY-ERRATUM-1.md`, "Outcome of run 40688572522".
GitHub runs a `run:` step under `bash -e`. The old `Certify` step therefore stopped at a
non-zero `assemble`, before the `CERTIFY_ADMISSION` and `CERTIFY_PLAN_PARAMS` lines and
the `.sha256` sidecar were written.

## The change (`full_space_certify_workflow.py`, the `Certify` step only)

1. **`-e` off for the whole step.** It runs under `set +e`, and `assemble` writes straight
   to the receipt, with its status taken as `rc`.
2. **Every line written on every outcome.** The step always writes `CERTIFY_ADMISSION`,
   `CERTIFY_PLAN_PARAMS`, a new line
   `CERTIFY_EXIT|assemble_rc=…|verdict_pass_lines=…`, and then the sidecar.
3. **Exit decided from the receipt.** The step exits 0 only when `assemble` returned 0
   **and** the receipt has exactly one `CERTIFY_VERDICT|PASS|` line. `INCONCLUSIVE`,
   `MISMATCH`, a crash with no verdict line, and a `PASS` line from a non-zero `assemble`
   all fail the step.

Nothing else changes. Given the same inputs, a workflow generated by the fixed generator
differs from the committed 3×3 certify workflow only in the `Certify` step.

## What is not changed

- **The committed 3×3 workflow and receipt.** Neither is regenerated or edited. The sealed
  receipt `CERTIFY-3X3-FULLSPACE.hbp` was produced by workflow commit `a5a991f`, which
  its `CERTIFY_PLAN_PARAMS` line records. It stays as it is.
- **Stage B and the certify step.** Neither is re-run.

under `bash -e`, in a scratch workspace. There, `full_space_certify.py` is a stub that
