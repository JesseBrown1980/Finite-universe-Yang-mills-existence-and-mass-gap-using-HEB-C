8. STOP RULES AND THE PER-DEPTH VERDICT RULE (HBP row 131)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The r = 9 run builds depths 2-5 at once. Its verdict depth is 5, its deepest depth. Depths 2, 3 and 4 are ladder
entries. (A fault run's verdict depth is its own deepest depth: 4 for the r = 12 CI runs, 5 at r = 9.) The checks
fall into two kinds.
- Run-integrity checks: FREEZE_PIN, CHI_TABLE, O_GAMMA_INVARIANT, O_DIAGONAL, H1_DETAILED_BALANCE,
  H1_TWO_SIDED_EXTERIOR, MOVES72, C0_EXTERIOR_COMPLETE, CENSUS, BURNSIDE_TOTAL, XCHECK_LIFT, FLOOR_PIN, COND_TAG,
  LO_LE_UP, SQ_BOUNDS, A_FROM_THEOREM, REPLAY_CPP, SCOPE_GUARD, EXT_REGEN and SIGN_POLAR (20). They are evaluated
  at every depth and in every route where they apply (EXT_REGEN and SIGN_POLAR at the verdict depth). Any FAIL
  refuses the whole run (REFUSE) and stops it. The FAIL takes effect at the end of the program step in which it
  occurs, after every check of that step has printed its CHECK row (no abort before the rows are printed), and a
  fault run always reaches the step that runs its named check (HBP header line 10, row 131). The one exception is
  FREEZE_PIN (E1, D1; HBP rows 030 and 113): it is a program step of its own, the first step of the engine and of the
  driver, and its FAIL, once its own CHECK row is printed, stops the program at once, before any other step or any
  arithmetic, in every run, fault runs included; neither clause ever delays it, and PF17 is refused by that FAIL. The
  only sub-row of any of them that may be NOT_EVALUATED is A_a_lo_le_raw under SQ_BOUNDS, where the F_GAP gate
  withheld its input; it is reported and is not a FAIL, and SQ_BOUNDS passes iff every evaluated sub-row passes and
  at least one is evaluated. A_FROM_THEOREM judges only rows labelled BOUND (section 2, D5), so a ladder-depth or
  INFORMATIVE row never fails it. Schedule by name (HBP row
  131): the r = 9 run and every r = 9 fault run execute all 20; every other run executes all except XCHECK_LIFT, so
  BURNSIDE_TOTAL runs in every run, on that run's own declared orbit field. Assembly: route verdicts, the reported
  verdict, the owner items and the BOUND label are assigned in the last job of the run, after REPLAY_CPP, EXT_REGEN,
  SIGN_POLAR and BURNSIDE_TOTAL, and (at r = 9) also XCHECK_LIFT.
- Route certificate checks: I0, I1_UP, I1_LO, I2_UP, I2_LO, I3_ODD, TAU2_ABOVE_UPSILON2, WINDOWS, G_WINDOW,
  H_WINDOW, Y_PD, NUBAR_PD, N1P, N2P, N2M, F_GAP, ADJ_CONSISTENCY and A_POS. They are evaluated at every depth, per
  route.
  * At a ladder depth, each outcome is reported as PASS, FAIL or NOT_EVALUATED. It never stops the run, never sets
    a verdict, and never reports a bound. The smoke already shows F_GAP failing at depth 2 (F2's Lemma-7 sine is
    3.49 at r = 10.5 and 1.10 at r = 12), so a literal "any REFUSE stops the run" would end the r = 9 run at depth 2
    and never reach depth 5. This rule exists to prevent that.
  * At the verdict depth, each route gets one verdict. It is REFUSE if a check with on_fail = REFUSE fails, or if
  any
    route certificate check is missing or NOT_EVALUATED. Otherwise it is HOLD if a check with on_fail = HOLD fails.
    Otherwise it is PASS.
- The reported verdict is the verdict-depth verdict of the reported route R (HBP row 094: primary, unless X7 set the
  secondary; R is fixed before any r = 9 run and never changed by an r = 9 outcome). Nothing is retuned. The frozen
  table (HBP row 131, verdict_table), for every pair of (primary, secondary) verdicts at depth 5:

    verdict of R    reported verdict   BOUND row               owner item 2   owner item 3 (HBP row 068)
    PASS            PASS               a_lo^2 of R, min form   PASS           PASS if R is the primary; HOLD if R is
                                                                              the secondary
    HOLD            HOLD               none                    HOLD           PASS if R is the primary (a HOLD means
                                                                              no listed check failed); HOLD if R is
                                                                              the secondary
    REFUSE          REFUSE             none                    REFUSE         REFUSE if one of its listed checks
                                                                              fails, else as in the HOLD line

  The other route's verdict (PASS, HOLD or REFUSE) never changes a column: its a_lo, a_hi and nu0 are printed
  INFORMATIVE with its route verdict, for the CONJECTURE table and the owner, and are never a bound. So, with the
  primary reported, primary HOLD and secondary PASS gives HOLD with no BOUND row, and primary REFUSE and secondary
  PASS gives REFUSE with no BOUND row; in both cases the secondary a_lo is printed INFORMATIVE. Owner item 1 (HBP
  row 066) uses checks that depend on X and G only, so both routes give it the same outcome.
- HOLD at depth 5 in both routes: report HOLD. The depth-6 fallback runs only once E8 exists and has passed X6
  (HBP row 094). It is a new run, and its verdict depth is 6. Its E3 - E2 cross-check (D7 E3LO, HBP row 140) is
  evaluated at depth 6, when Theorem I passes there.
- An exact selection rule that makes the amplitude 0 is reported as a dark result and HOLD (PR #148 row 19). It is
  never forced positive.
- A CONJECTURE row that fails is reported FALSIFIED. Theorems and verdicts are unchanged. A CONJECTURE row whose
  measured quantity is NOT_EVALUATED or missing is FALSIFIED, except where the row itself predicts NOT_EVALUATED
  (row 088's depth-2 clause and row 133).
